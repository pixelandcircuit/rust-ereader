Welcome to the E-Reader

This device reads EPUB, HTML, and plain-text files from an SD card. Here is a quick guide to the controls.

Turning Pages

Use the two physical buttons on the side of the device to navigate. The top button goes forward one page; the bottom button goes back one page. Hold either button for one second to enter fast-scroll mode, where the page advances automatically while you hold it down. Release to jump to the displayed page.

Opening a Book

Tap the Library button in the top bar to open the book browser. Select a title from the list and tap Read to open it. Books must be placed on the SD card before powering on the device.

Settings

Tap the Settings button in the top bar to open the settings panel. From there you can adjust:

  • Font Size: Small, Medium, or Large.
  • Backlight: Off, Low, or High.
  • Orientation: Portrait, Landscape, Reverse Portrait, or Reverse Landscape.

Syncing the Time

Tap Sync Time in the settings panel to connect to Wi-Fi and fetch the current time from the network. The time is shown in the top bar.

Cleaning the Screen

Over time, faint ghost lines may appear on the display from repeated page turns. Tap Clean Screen in the settings panel to run a full cleaning cycle that resets every pixel to a clear state. The screen will flash black and white a few times; this is normal.

Saving Your Place

Your reading position is saved automatically whenever you turn a page or close a book. The next time you power on the device it will open to the same page.
